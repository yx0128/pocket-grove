# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Pocket Grove to be a calm, welcoming project. We pledge to make participation harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that builds a good space:

- Being kind and patient with questions, especially from newer contributors.
- Giving specific, actionable feedback about code and design.
- Respecting different levels of experience and different working styles.
- Accepting responsibility, apologizing, and learning from mistakes.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory jokes.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption, trolling, or bad-faith arguments.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when representing the project in public spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, social media, events).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this code. They may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Report concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository’s security/contact channel rather than in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly; maintainers will respect the privacy and safety of the reporter.
